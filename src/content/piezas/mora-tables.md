MORA explores the relationship between order and natural variation. Built from an orthogonal grid, each piece combines different wood species, allowing their unique grain, tone, and texture to shape the composition. The contrast between geometry and the organic character of wood creates a quiet visual rhythm that feels both familiar and unexpected.

Crafted from solid wood, each piece reveals its natural grain, tonal variation, and organic markings. These characteristics are not imperfections, but expressions of the material itself.
